At the metabolic level U ≡ 0: the law is held fixed and the system merely

is therefore at the level below.

## Selection level (added 2026-08-01)

Selection and heredity live at the U layer — propagation modifying propagation
across generations. The formal entry point is **adaptive dynamics**: the fast
layer is ecology (population densities relaxing to the attractor of a frozen
trait), the slow layer is trait change, and the object coupling them is the
**invasion fitness** s(φ, φ′) — the growth rate of a rare variant trait φ′ in a
resident population φ, with s(φ, φ) = 0.

| 𝓜 | Adaptive dynamics |
|---|---|
| X | population densities / ecological state |
| F_φ | ecological dynamics at frozen trait φ |
| A(φ) | ecological attractor of the resident |
| U | canonical equation, U(φ) = ∇_{φ′} s(φ, φ′) \|_{φ′=φ} |
| singular point | U(φ\*) = 0 |

What this made visible is a *constraint on U*, not a new biological fact.
Because s has **two** arguments, a singular point carries two independent
second-order quantities — convergence stability (∂₁∂₂s + ∂₂²s < 0) and
uninvadability (∂₂²s < 0) — and their disagreement is what produces
**evolutionary branching**. **P-10** shows that a U built from a one-argument
functional collapses the two into one and can never branch. The two-argument
form of U is therefore not a modelling convenience here; it is what the
phenomenon requires.

This is the first place the U layer has done load-bearing work, and it is why
OP-9's "self-modification" question now has a checkable form: *does the law
layer evaluate counterfactual laws, or only its own attractor?*

## What this mapping does NOT establish

A mapping is an interpretation, never evidence. Nothing in this file supports
promoting any concept.

In particular: the adaptive-dynamics correspondence is a *reading* of 𝓜 in
biological vocabulary, not a proof that 𝓜 captures evolution. The metabolic and
selection levels above are two different readings of the same symbols and are
not claimed to compose. The transferable content sits in P-10, which is a
statement about 𝓜 and needs no biology; the biology supplied the counterexample
that motivated it. No OP-16 novelty is claimed here: the classification of
singular points is textbook (Metz–Geritz–Nisbet; Geritz–Kisdi–Meszéna–Metz
1998; Dieckmann–Law), and exporting it outside biology is itself prior art
(Dercole–Dieckmann–Obersteiner–Rinaldi 2008, technology and markets).
