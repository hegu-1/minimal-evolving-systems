the NS mapping shows redistribution is B-neutral (⟨(u·∇)u, u⟩ = 0) and
P-1's H2 bounds amplification. **Fix (Writer's split):**

  dB/dt = J_in + J_amp + J_redist − J_diss,  with J_redist B-neutral by
  definition (⟨N_r(δ), δ⟩ = 0 under the pairing defining B).

bounds systems by their J_amp (new remark R5 in `proofs/`). This gives
OP-14's "Amplification ∼ Dissipation" complexity condition a precise meaning
for the first time.
