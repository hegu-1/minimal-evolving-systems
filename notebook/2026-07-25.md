## Sixth step — P-2: local well-posedness of the coupled layers

The OP-18 route sketched above, executed. **P-2** filed with complete proof
(`proofs/p002_local_wellposedness.md`): under a continuous embedding of the
law space 𝓖 into Lipschitz vector fields (evaluation bounds E1–E2) and a
locally Lipschitz U, the two-layer system has unique local C¹ solutions,
and the derived propagation Φ_{t,s} exists globally on the interval with the
process property. Technique: Picard–Lindelöf on the product space; entirely
classical (R5), no novelty.

Two structural consequences, larger than the proposition itself:

1. **The suspension objection is now a formal fact, not a philosophical
   worry (R2 → OP-17).** The proof constructs an *autonomous* semiflow on
   X × 𝓖 — the static presentation is canonical. The evolving-presentation
   operators Φ_{t,s} exist only relative to a realized law trajectory
   (changing δ₀ changes the law curve). Any canonical-split proposal must
   explain away this asymmetry, or D3 stays presentation-relative.
2. **OP-18 splits cleanly**: ambient-space well-posedness DONE (P-2);
   remaining (i) K-invariance — a Nagumo-type tangency condition on U at
   ∂K; (ii) global existence — a two-layer balance estimate (P-1-style on
   the δ-layer plus a growth bound on ‖F_t‖_𝓖). Both are well-scoped
   Formalizer tasks.

## Seventh step — P-3: the lead verified and proved; OP-14 reframed

**P-3** filed with complete proof
(`proofs/p003_equilibrium_in_absorbing_region.md`):

(a) rest-point theorem — a compact convex positively invariant set of a
locally Lipschitz flow in ℝⁿ contains an equilibrium (Brouwer fixed points
of Φ_{t_n}, t_n ↓ 0, accumulate on a rest point);
(b) under P-1's hypotheses every sublevel ball {B ≤ c},
c > γ/(2(α−β)), is compact, convex, and positively invariant (boundary
derivative strictly negative), so it contains an equilibrium.

**OP-14 reframed accordingly**: the absorbing region can never be
equilibrium-free in finite dimension; the gate to persistent complexity is
*every equilibrium unstable + a non-trivial attracting set*, not equilibrium
absence. The search space is halved: attention shifts to instability
conditions on the linearization at the (necessarily existing) equilibria.

Open threads left by P-3: infinite-dimensional extension via Schauder for
