**The battery, in repo coordinates:**

1. *Is the decomposition mathematically non-arbitrary?* = OP-17. Current
   state: P-2 makes the product (suspended) presentation canonical and the
   evolving presentation trajectory-relative. Non-arbitrariness, if it exists,
   must take the form of a **dynamically characterized fibration** of the
   suspended space (state-fibers over a law-base), unique under stated
   invariance/extremality conditions — not a primitive split.
2. *Can every law update be absorbed into a larger state space?* = the
   suspension objection, already formalized. Yes, always (skew-product /
   autonomous extension). What absorption preserves is single-trajectory
   prediction; what it erases is **quantification over counterfactual laws**.
3. *What phenomena require the split?* = P-10/P-11 territory. "Inexpressible"
   is too strong; the defensible form is minimality: any phenomenon *defined*
   by counterfactual-law comparison (invadability, branching under frequency
   dependence) is not a property of a single suspended trajectory. P-10 is
   this point in theorem form: one-argument U provably cannot diversify.
4. *Historical precedents for primitive-change?* One usable observation, new
   to the repo: correct primitive-changes have a fingerprint — **they make
   impossibility theorems provable** (Turing → halting; Kolmogorov → Bertrand
   dissolved; Shannon → converse coding bounds; Arrow; population thinking in
   Darwin). This independently supports the 2026-08-02 reframe (foundational
   question class → impossibility/minimality payload). The guard recorded
   with that reframe remains in force: the pattern earns nothing by itself.
5. *Nearest frameworks?* All already on file or in references: skew-product /
   cocycle flows (absorption, exogenous driving, no endogenous U); adaptive
   dynamics (two-argument invasion fitness — closest contact, per P-10);
   meta-learning / bilevel (two layers, fixed outer objective — sits inside
   P-10's hypothesis); viability theory (inside P-4); slaving / singular
   perturbation (split as timescale — quantitative, not ontological); Rosen's
   (M,R)-systems and Ashby's ultrastability (endogenous law-change ancestors,
   the latter a literal U with a viability trigger, 1952); causal states /
   PSR / Mori–Zwanzig (mappings/cognition.md).
6. *If misguided, where does the mistake begin?* Three candidate origin
   points, in increasing depth: (a) at the word "primitive" — suspension
   forbids object-level primitivity; the salvage is canonical structure
