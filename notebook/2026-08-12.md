## OP-17 in Erlangen form (Writer, DISCUSSION)

Maintainer pushed the fingerprint question one level down: is there a general,
formalizable criterion separating legitimate primitives from convenient
decompositions? The classical toolkit answers component-wise, and each
component sharpens OP-17.

**The three formalizable components.** (i) *Invariance* (Klein/Erlangen): a
legitimate primitive is an invariant of the theory's own group of admissible
re-presentations; a non-invariant primitive is gauge — demoted to perspective,
not refuted (absolute simultaneity, the ether's rest frame). (ii)
*Independence* (Padoa–Beth): Beth definability — a concept implicitly defined
by the theory is explicitly definable, hence redundant as a primitive (force
in GR, demoted to a theorem); Padoa's method certifies genuine novelty by a
pair of models agreeing on the old primitives and disagreeing on the new one
(the independence of the parallel postulate). (iii) *Closure* (representation
/ uniqueness theorems): impossibility theorems quantify over a totality, so
they become provable exactly when the primitive closes its class (Church–
Turing as thesis, Kolmogorov's axioms, Shannon's uniqueness + coding
converse). A decomposition that can fit anything (epicycles = a universal
approximation basis) supports no impossibility statement; **convenience fits,
ontology forbids.** The criterion formalizes only *relative* to a declared
phenomenon class and re-presentation group — which is the shape C-001 v2 /
OP-3 (ambient class 𝒞) already has; the choice of class and group is the
non-formalizable residue.

**The sharpening.** OP-17 currently asks "can the state/law split be made
canonical?" Erlangen form: *first fix the admissible group of
re-presentations of an evolving system — the suspension map is an element —
then primitives are its invariants; the split is legitimate iff some
invariant separates law-directions from state-directions independently of
presentation.* Candidate invariant: the P-10 obstruction. If "no
diversification without counterfactual evaluation" retains a statement under
every admissible re-presentation, the line it draws is invariant — the
impossibility theorem itself is the certificate of the primitive's
legitimacy, which is *why* the fingerprint of §1 item 4 works as a criterion.

**Honest caveat (the tower).** Every level's split can be absorbed into the
next level's larger flow (population-level branching is again one flow), so
the real question is whether anything stays invariant along the whole tower
of absorptions. This is the depth at which OP-17 actually lives; recorded so
the next round attacks the tower, not the single rung.
