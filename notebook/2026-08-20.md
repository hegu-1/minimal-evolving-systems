### Setup: the two-argument functional and its identity with the joint loss

The per-expert loss share of P-11 R1, made explicit as a two-argument
functional: for a variant expert at φ′ routed against a resident at φ,

  ℓ(φ, φ′) = E_y[ g′(y) · (y − φ′)² ],
  g′(y) = ς( (φ′ − φ)(y − (φ+φ′)/2) / τ ),   y ~ N(0, σ²),

with ς the logistic function. Two exact structural identities:

- **Joint-loss decomposition.** L(φ₁, φ₂) = ℓ(φ₂, φ₁) + ℓ(φ₁, φ₂): the P-11
  joint loss is the symmetrisation of ℓ.
- **P-10 class in this notation.** Resident-independent fitness (P-10's
  hypothesis (A)) ⟺ ℓ is a function of φ′ alone ⟺ ∂₁ℓ ≡ 0. The control arm
  of P-11(v) is exactly this class: ℓ_ctrl(φ, φ′) = ½(σ² + φ′²).

### The second-order structure at the collapsed point

All derivatives at (0, 0); each uses ς′(0) = 1/4, ς″(0) = 0, and Gaussian
moments E[y²] = σ², under the same domination argument as P-11 (ii). For ℓ:

  ∂₁²ℓ = σ²/4τ,   ∂₁∂₂ℓ = σ²/2τ,   ∂₂²ℓ = 1 − 5σ²/4τ,

with the sum-rule check (∂₁² + 2∂₁∂₂ + ∂₂²)ℓ = 1, forced by
ℓ(φ, φ) = ½(σ² + φ²). For the joint loss, via the decomposition identity:

  a := ∂₁²L = ∂₁²ℓ + ∂₂²ℓ = 1 − σ²/τ,
  b := ∂₁∂₂L = 2 ∂₁∂₂ℓ = σ²/τ.

Expert-exchange symmetry forces the Hessian eigenvectors to be (1,1) and
(1,−1) — the isotypic decomposition of the ℤ₂ relabelling representation —
with eigenvalues

  λ_even = a + b = **1**   (τ-independent: routing cannot destabilise the
  mean; this is P-11's ∂²_u L = 2, halved),
  λ_odd = a − b = **1 − 2σ²/τ**   (P-11's ∂²_v L = 2(1 − 2σ²/τ), halved).

All six values confirmed by quadrature at σ ∈ {1, 1.3} across
τ ∈ [0.5, 5], agreement ≲ 1e−5, sign change of a − b landing exactly at
τ = 2σ² in both cases.

### Half 1 — the candidate as stated dies

The 2026-08-12 candidate was the *bare* defect: the frequency-dependence
mixed partial itself. It is **sign-definite**: ∂₁∂₂ℓ = σ²/2τ > 0 for every
τ, σ. It never changes sign, at 2σ² or anywhere else. Moreover the classical
invasion-fitness readings of ℓ scatter their thresholds elsewhere: with
s(φ, φ′) = ℓ(φ, φ) − ℓ(φ, φ′), convergence stability of the collapsed point
flips at τ = 3σ²/4 and ESS/invadability at τ = 5σ²/4; a frozen-resident
reading s = L(φ, φ) − L(φ′, φ) flips at τ = σ². Three natural constructions,
three thresholds — none of them 2σ², and none of them the sign change of the
mixed partial. The bare two-argument data is construction-relative, which is
precisely the non-canonicity OP-17 worries about; nothing built directly on
it survives re-presentation.

A second, separate death: the *name* was wrong. The 2026-08-12 parenthesis
("diversity needs curl") sat in tension with P-10 R1, and the computation
settles it: the P-11 slow flow is a gradient flow (P-11 R1 — a potential
game; its curl/harmonic component is identically zero) and it splits anyway.
At the law layer, the defect that gates diversity is the **symmetric
off-diagonal coupling** b, not any antisymmetric part of the vector field.
"Antisymmetric" survives only in the isotypic sense: the unstable direction
is the exchange-**odd** one.

### Half 2 — the forced repair, which succeeds exactly

What does change sign at τ = 2σ², and only there, is the **exchange-odd
curvature**

  λ_odd = a − b = (self-curvature) − (coupling defect),

equivalently, through the decomposition identity, the anti-diagonal curvature
of the two-argument functional itself:

  λ_odd = (∂₁² − 2∂₁∂₂ + ∂₂²) ℓ |_(0,0),

the curvature of the per-expert loss along the counterfactual direction
"variant and resident move apart symmetrically". So the invariant is not the
defect; it is the defect **measured against the self-term**. Split ⟺ b > a:
coupling overtakes self-curvature.

This repair is not free — it is forced, and it strengthens the P-10
connection into a two-level statement:

- **Defect ≠ 0 is necessary** (P-10, joint form): on the resident-independent
  class ∂₁ℓ ≡ 0, so ∂₁∂₂ℓ = ∂₁²ℓ = 0, hence λ_odd = λ_even — the
  exchange-odd and exchange-even curvatures *coincide*. A
  symmetry-breaking-only instability (odd unstable, even stable — branching
  rather than runaway) is therefore impossible in the P-10 class. This is
  P-10's empty branching class recovered as a Hessian identity.
- **Defect > self-term is the threshold** (P-11): b = σ²/τ against
  a = 1 − σ²/τ gives τ* = 2σ².

**First computed value of the (repaired) tower invariant:
λ_odd = 1 − 2σ²/τ.** In OP-20's exit taxonomy this is evidence for exit (c)
shading into (a): the invariant exists and is computable *relative to the
declared group* — here expert relabelling ℤ₂, whose isotypic decomposition is
what makes λ_odd well-defined — exactly the relativised form the Erlangen
entry predicted the answer would take.

### What this changes upstream

1. **OP-20's candidate is re-specified.** Not "the non-gradient
   (antisymmetric) defect" but: *the curvature gap on the exchange-odd
   isotypic component — off-diagonal coupling relative to diagonal
   self-curvature.* The bare mixed partial is not an invariant; the isotypic
   curvature is.
2. **OP-19 sharpens into a two-irreducible hypothesis.** The off-diagonal
   interaction structure of a layer decomposes into an antisymmetric
   (rotational) part and a symmetric (coupling) part, and the two known
   obstruction theorems sit on *different irreducibles of the same object*:
   - state layer (P-5 side): if the symmetrised Jacobian is negative definite,
     no periodic orbits (Bendixson–Demidovich / contraction); recurrence
     requires the skew part to survive the symmetric damping;
   - law layer (P-10/P-11 side): if the functional is separable, no
     diversity; branching requires the coupling to beat the self-curvature —
     threshold computed above.
   Both rungs now have the same shape — *interesting behaviour begins where
   the off-diagonal part overtakes the diagonal dissipative part* — with the
   antisymmetric irreducible gating recurrence and the symmetric irreducible
   gating diversity. P-11 is the computed instance on the symmetric side.
   If OP-19 has a unification, this is now its precise candidate form; a
   literal "same tensor at both rungs" is dead (P-11 has zero curl and
   branches).
3. **D3's repair path updates.** 2026-08-12 proposed *genuinely evolving :=
   defect non-zero in every admissible presentation*. In repaired form:
   genuinely evolving := the exchange-odd curvature gap (coupling minus
   self-term, in whatever isotypic decomposition the admissible group forces)
   is negative in every admissible presentation — a threshold condition, not
   a non-vanishing condition.
